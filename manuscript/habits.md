# Recommended Habits

Consider following habits for adoption in your everday life.

## don't hesitate to anchor

Practice in easy conditions, advance to more difficult (not dangerous) conditions, then you will be prepared for anchoring also in challenging conditions.

## practice sailing maneuvers to perfection

Tacking, stopping the boat, sailing slowly, drifting backwards, picking up speed again... these maneuvers are fundamental for approaching narrow spaces such as anchorages or marinas. The better your sailing skills, the more confident you can approach these moorings without depending on a (strong) engine.
